FAQs

Before your visit.

A few simple answers to help you feel prepared and comfortable before arriving at Angel Spa.

Please arrive about 5–10 minutes early so you have time to settle in, ask questions, and begin your session feeling relaxed.

If you have an injury, recent surgery, or medical concern, please consult your healthcare provider first and let us know before your session begins.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please call as soon as possible so we can adjust the appointment time.

Parking is typically available near the location. Please allow a few extra minutes during busy shopping or traffic hours.
